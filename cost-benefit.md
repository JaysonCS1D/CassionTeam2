# Cost-Benefit Analysis

## Project: Python Task Manager App

---

## 1. Development Cost

| Item                       | Hours | Rate ($/hr) | Cost     |
|----------------------------|-------|-------------|----------|
| Backend development        | 20    | $25         | $500     |
| Testing and QA             | 8     | $20         | $160     |
| Documentation              | 6     | $15         | $90      |
| Project management         | 4     | $20         | $80      |
| **Total Development Cost** |       |             | **$830** |

---

## 2. Operational Cost (Per Year)

| Item                        | Cost per Month | Cost per Year |
|-----------------------------|----------------|---------------|
| Hosting (Render.com free)   | $0             | $0            |
| Domain name (optional)      | $1             | $12           |
| Maintenance and updates     | $50            | $600          |
| **Total Operational Cost**  |                | **$612**      |

---

## 3. Tangible Benefits

| Benefit                           | Estimated Value per Year |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Time saved on task tracking       | $1,200                   |
| Reduced missed deadlines          | $800                     |
| Improved team productivity        | $1,500                   |
| **Total Tangible Benefits**       | **$3,500**               |

---

## 4. Intangible Benefits

- Improved team organization and communication
- Better visibility into project progress at a glance
- Reduced stress from forgotten or missed tasks
- Academic learning value for the development team
- Portfolio project demonstrating software engineering skills
- Reusable foundation for future scalable products

---

## 5. ROI Calculation

```
Total Investment (Year 1) = Development Cost + Operational Cost
                          = $830 + $612
                          = $1,442

Net Benefit (Year 1) = Tangible Benefits - Total Investment
                     = $3,500 - $1,442
                     = $2,058

ROI = (Net Benefit / Total Investment) x 100
    = ($2,058 / $1,442) x 100
    = 142.7%
```

**Return on Investment: 142.7%**

---

## 6. Recommendation

**Proceed with the project.**

The Task Manager App delivers a strong ROI of 142.7% in Year 1.
Development costs are low, hosting is free on Render.com, and productivity
benefits significantly outweigh the total investment.

From Year 2 onwards, only operational costs remain ($612/year) while
benefits continue, making the cumulative ROI even stronger over time.
